    - **Total (`12,459` positives):** `10,870` (`87.25%`) from `FAILURE` + `1,589` (`12.75%`) from `ASSISTANCE`.

#### D. Methodological Assessment of Re-Running `train_evaluate.py` After Fixing `SimpleImputer(keep_empty_features=True)` (`Finding M-4`)
- `reports/PROJECT_LOG.md:489-491` notes that during final verification, `SimpleImputer(strategy="median")` was updated to `SimpleImputer(strategy="median", keep_empty_features=True)` (`src/features/preprocessing_pipeline.py:400`) and `train_evaluate.py` was re-executed, changing `LightGBM_CAMELS` Test PR-AUC from `0.6393` to `0.6454` (`+0.0061`) while CV Mean PR-AUC remained `0.3627`.
- **Forensic Verification of Why Test PR-AUC Changed:**
  1. Why was `keep_empty_features=True` needed? In Purged CV Folds 1 and 2 (`Train_1 <= 1989` and `Train_2 <= 1993`), Basel risk-based capital (`total_rbc_ratio`) and `pastdue30_89_to_loans` are 100% `NaN` in Fold 1 (`Train <= 1989`). Without `keep_empty_features=True`, `sklearn`'s `SimpleImputer` silently dropped those columns in Fold 1 (`88` output columns instead of `90`), emitting a `UserWarning`.
  2. Why did the full-`DEV` (`1984–2006`) fit change when no numeric column is 100% `NaN` on full `DEV`? Between the initial run and the re-run, `src/features/build_features.py:147-150` standardized empty strings `""` and `null` in `BKCLASS` into a single `"MISSING"` category, reducing the `OneHotEncoder` output on `DEV` from `11` categories (`50` total transformed features) to `10` categories (`49` total transformed features: `39` numeric + `10` `BKCLASS` dummy columns).
  3. Because `LightGBM_CAMELS` uses `colsample_bytree = 0.8` (`src/models/train_evaluate.py:488`), changing the total feature dimension from `50` (`int(0.8 * 50) = 40` features sampled per tree) to `49` (`int(0.8 * 49) = 39` features sampled per tree) altered the pseudo-random column subsampling sequence during tree growth on `DEV`, shifting `LightGBM_CAMELS` Test PR-AUC from `0.6393` to `0.6454` (while deterministic models without random column subsampling—`Supervisory_Rule_Baseline` `0.4662`, `Logistic_L2` `0.5882`, `Logistic_ElasticNet` `0.5880`—remained bit-for-bit identical!).
- **Assessment:** Neither the `keep_empty_features=True` bugfix nor the `BKCLASS` empty-string cleanup was tuned on or motivated by Test performance; however, because `train_evaluate.py` bundles CV and Test evaluation into a single script, fixing a preprocessing bug automatically re-evaluates `TEST`. The final report should state this cleanly as a software-hygiene note in Part 4/5.

#### E. Economic Evaluation of Cost $C_{\text{FN}}$ (`$2,599.75k`) & Bank-Quarter vs Distinct-Bank Accounting (`Finding M-5`)
- **How $C_{\text{FN}}$ is Computed (`src/models/train_evaluate.py:559-568`):**
  - The code filters `gold_feature_table.parquet` to `(split_regime == "dev") & (target_fail_4q == 1) & (_meta_fail_cost > 0)` (`N = 9,880` positive bank-quarters across `2,592` distinct failed `CERT`s), computes the median `_meta_fail_cost` across those `9,880` bank-quarters (`$10,399.00k`), and divides by `4` to obtain `$2,599.75k` per positive bank-quarter (yielding $C_{\text{FN}} / C_{\text{FP}} = \$2,599.75\text{k} / \$50.00\text{k} \approx 52:1$).
- **Critical Economic Audit of This Calculation:**
  1. **Bank-Quarter Median vs Distinct-Bank Median (`$10,399.00k` vs `$9,744.69k`):** Because each failed bank appears in up to 4 positive quarters (`9,880 / 2,592 = 3.81` positive quarters per failed bank in `DEV`), taking the median over *bank-quarters* weights banks with 4 full quarters of pre-failure filings `4x` as heavily as rapid-failure banks with only 1 quarter. Independent calculation on `DEV` shows the **distinct-bank** median DIF loss is **`$9,744.69k`** (mean `$69,023.78k`) across `2,592` failed banks. Dividing the distinct-bank median by the average positive quarters per failed bank (`$9,744.69k / 3.8117 = $2,556.51k`) differs from `$10,399.00k / 4 = $2,599.75k` by only **`1.66%`** (`$43.24k`), so the `52:1` cost ratio and optimal threshold $\tau^* = 0.0401$ are virtually unaffected.
  2. **Interpretation of Net Savings (`$4,074.08M` on `TEST`):** On `TEST` (`2008Q1`–`2014Q4`), there are `2,052` positive bank-quarters across `526` distinct failed `CERT`s (`avg = 3.90` quarters/bank), whose actual total DIF resolution loss was **`$70,453.90 Million` (`$70.45 Billion`)** (distinct-bank median = `$39,566.82k`, mean = `$133,942.77k`). At $\tau^* = 0.0401$, `LightGBM_CAMELS` flags `TP = 1,732` of `2,052` positive bank-quarters (`84.41%` recall) and `FP = 8,579` false positives (`9,426` total flags = `4.43%` of `TEST` bank-quarters).
  3. **Two Caveats Required in Final Report:**
     - **Loss-Mitigation Fraction ($\alpha$):** Computing net savings as `TP * C_FN - FP * C_FP = $4,074.08M` (`src/models/train_evaluate.py:708-710`) implicitly assumes that an early on-site examination **avoids 100% ($\alpha = 1.0$) of the DIF resolution loss**, whereas in regulatory practice early intervention (prompt corrective action, dividend restrictions, forced recapitalization, or earlier closure) only mitigates a fraction $\alpha \in [20\%, 50\%]$ of the ultimate DIF loss. However, because false-positive audit cost is only `FP * $50k = $428.95M` (vs `TP * C_FN = $4,502.77M` gross avoided cost at `DEV` median, or even larger at `TEST` GFC median `$39,567k / 4 = $9,892k` per quarter), the model remains massively cost-saving for any mitigation fraction $\alpha > 9.5\%$!
     - **Repeat Quarterly Flags per Bank:** Flagging the same deteriorating bank in 4 consecutive quarters (`Q-4, Q-3, Q-2, Q-1`) triggers a single supervisory enforcement lifecycle rather than 4 independent `$50k` examinations or 4 independent loss-avoidance events. Dividing `COST` by 4 correctly normalizes the *benefit* side across the 4 quarters (`4 * (COST / 4) = COST`), and the report should explicitly explain this quarterly amortization logic.

#### B. Verification & Regulatory Explanation of `Supervisory_Rule_Baseline` (`0.4662` Test PR-AUC vs `0.2361` Dev OOF PR-AUC)

1. **Exact Numerical Verification (`src/models/train_evaluate.py:85-133`):**
   - `SupervisoryRuleBaseline` extracts the winsorized, median-imputed, standardized `texas_ratio_proxy` column (`index 11` of `NUMERIC_FEATURES`) and applies a monotone logistic mapping $\hat{p} = \sigma(\text{clip}(z, -20, 20))$.

2. **Why Does `texas_ratio_proxy` Jump from `0.2361` DEV PR-AUC to `0.4662` TEST PR-AUC?**
   - Independent empirical stratification on `gold_feature_table.parquet` reveals a profound structural shift in US bank resolution policy between `DEV` (`1984–2006`, S&L Crisis) and `TEST` (`2008–2014`, Global Financial Crisis):
     - **In `DEV` (`1984–2006`, Pre-FDICIA Regulatory Forbearance):**
       - `texas_ratio_proxy >= 1.0` (`100%`): `50,890` bank-quarters $\rightarrow$ 4Q failure precision = **`16.63%`**
       - `texas_ratio_proxy >= 2.0` (`200%`): `26,997` bank-quarters $\rightarrow$ 4Q failure precision = **`25.69%`**
       - `texas_ratio_proxy >= 5.0` (`500%`): `16,343` bank-quarters $\rightarrow$ 4Q failure precision = **`28.83%`**
     - **In `TEST` (`2008–2014`, Post-FDICIA Prompt Corrective Action):**
       - `texas_ratio_proxy >= 1.0` (`100%`): `6,821` bank-quarters $\rightarrow$ 4Q failure precision = **`23.52%`**
       - `texas_ratio_proxy >= 2.0` (`200%`): `2,224` bank-quarters $\rightarrow$ 4Q failure precision = **`46.90%`**
       - `texas_ratio_proxy >= 5.0` (`500%`): `347` bank-quarters $\rightarrow$ 4Q failure precision = **`76.08%`**
   - **Economic Explanation for the Report:** During the 1980s S&L crisis (`DEV`), the FSLIC lacked the reserves to close insolvent thrifts and practiced widespread regulatory forbearance, allowing "zombie thrifts" with Texas Ratios exceeding `200%–500%` to remain open for 2 to 5 years before resolution (`target_fail_4q = 0` during the earlier years of insolvency, depressing 4Q precision to `25.7%–28.8%`). After Congress enacted the **Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation Improvement Act (FDICIA) of 1991**, **Prompt Corrective Action (PCA)** legally mandated that regulators close critically undercapitalized institutions (`tangible equity / assets <= 2%`) within 90 days. Consequently, during the 2008–2014 GFC (`TEST`), banks breaching `texas_ratio_proxy >= 200%` or `500%` were closed rapidly within the 4-quarter horizon (`46.9%` and `76.1%` precision), nearly doubling the single-ratio Texas Ratio PR-AUC from `0.2361` to `0.4662`! Even so, multivariate `LightGBM_CAMELS` (`0.6454` Test PR-AUC) outperforms the Texas Ratio by **`+0.1792` (`+38.4%` relative gain)** by integrating capital, earnings, liquidity (`bro_to_dep`), and 4Q trajectories.
